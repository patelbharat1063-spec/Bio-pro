Simulation library

Choose a NEET Biology model. Five models load detailed anatomical meshes from NIH 3D; remaining topics retain interactive study schematics. These are educational resources, not diagnostic or surgical models.

How to explore

  1. Choose a model from the library or drop-down.
  2. Drag the model to rotate it in 3D.
  3. Pinch or scroll to zoom in and out.
  4. Select a structure label for its NCERT-level function.
  5. Use Reset view to return to the starting angle.
Models are rendered in your browser. An internet connection is required to load the free Three.js graphics library.
